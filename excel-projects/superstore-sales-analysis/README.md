# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using Microsoft Excel.

The goal is to examine sales performance, profitability, product performance and regional differences in order to identify relevant business trends and potential areas for improvement.

## Business Questions

The analysis will focus on the following questions:

1. What are the total sales and total profit?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which product categories generate the highest sales and profit?
4. Which products generate the highest sales?
5. Which products generate the highest profit?
6. How do sales develop over time?
7. Are there noticeable seasonal patterns?
8. How do discounts relate to profitability?
9. Are there regions, categories or products with high sales but low profitability?
